# Market Intelligence

## Workflow

1. Define the market boundary: service categories, customer versus transaction definition, geography, period, channel, and whether spend is gross or net of commissions.
2. Map the value chain: brides, families, salons, independent artists, aggregators, product brands, venues, photographers, and payment/booking infrastructure.
3. Segment consumers by need and behaviour (for example city, budget, wedding format, decision-maker, lead time, trust concern, and service bundle). Do not use demographic labels without a behavioural implication.
4. Build a competitor grid from observable evidence: proposition, target segment, supply model, geographic reach, trust mechanisms, booking flow, pricing, reviews, partnerships, and monetisation.
5. Size TAM, SAM, and SOM bottom-up first. Show formula, inputs, source, date, unit, and confidence. Use top-down only as a cross-check.
6. Benchmark comparable metrics only when definitions and periods match. Mark non-comparable cells as `not disclosed`.
7. Translate findings into implications for acquisition, supply density, trust, take rate, retention, and execution sequence.

## Guardrails

Never turn a market-report headline into a Naturals-specific number without reconciling scope. Never infer revenue, market share, or profitability from app downloads, social followers, listings, or review counts. Label all estimates and provide a sensitivity range.

## Output contract

Deliver: market definition; ecosystem map; segment cards; competitor table; TAM/SAM/SOM bridge; benchmark caveats; opportunity spaces; implications and open questions. Use `templates/market-sizing.md`.
