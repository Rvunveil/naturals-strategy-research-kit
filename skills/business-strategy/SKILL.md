# Business Strategy

## Method

- Frame the problem as a measurable outcome, not a feature request.
- Use issue trees, customer jobs-to-be-done, value-chain analysis, Five Forces, capability/advantage mapping, and platform flywheel logic only when they clarify a decision.
- For a marketplace, explicitly address chicken-and-egg sequencing, local liquidity, trust and quality control, disintermediation, supply incentives, demand acquisition, and governance.
- Generate 3-5 strategic options. Score them against customer value, right to win, economics, feasibility, risk, and time to evidence. State the criteria and weights; do not hide judgement in a composite score.
- Recommend a focused beachhead, not an unlimited national launch. Define testable milestones, owners, dependencies, leading indicators, and kill/scale thresholds.
- Connect every recommendation to evidence and to an execution mechanism. Avoid generic “leverage AI”, “build partnerships”, or “improve experience” language.

## Output contract

Return: diagnosis; strategic choices and trade-offs; target segment and value proposition; platform design; defensibility; GTM; 0-90 day tests; 12-24 month roadmap; risks and mitigations; metrics; assumptions. Mark hypotheses that require research.
