# Critical Review

## Challenge sequence

1. Restate the recommendation and the decision it claims to improve.
2. Trace each material claim to evidence and flag unsupported leaps.
3. Attack the market definition, TAM/SAM/SOM bridge, competitor comparability, and India-specific assumptions.
4. Recalculate unit economics using downside inputs; test capacity, seasonality, CAC, take rate, refunds, trust costs, and disintermediation.
5. Challenge platform sequencing: who joins first, why they stay, how local liquidity forms, and what prevents off-platform leakage.
6. Test whether Naturals has a right to win versus salons, independent artists, marketplaces, social discovery, and incumbent relationships.
7. Identify operational, regulatory, privacy, safety, reputational, and technology risks.
8. Replace weak assertions with a smaller, testable claim. Give the cheapest decisive experiment and a kill/scale threshold.

## Output contract

Return: verdict; strongest evidence; strongest counterargument; fatal or material gaps; sensitivity results; judge questions; required fixes; decision gates. Do not reject an idea merely because data is missing—label the missing data and propose how to obtain it.
