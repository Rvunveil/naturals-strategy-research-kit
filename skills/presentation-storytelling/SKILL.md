# Presentation Storytelling

## Narrative

- Start with the decision and answer, then prove it. Build a single governing message.
- Use a situation-complication-question-answer arc or a pyramid structure; one slide should make one point.
- Sequence: ambition and problem, customer truth, market opportunity, competitive gap, strategic choice, business model, economics, roadmap, risks, ask.
- Put evidence beside the claim it supports. Label facts, estimates, assumptions, and hypotheses on the slide or in notes.
- Prefer charts, decision matrices, journey maps, funnels, and timelines over dense prose. Every visual must have a takeaway title.
- Never create a false precision chart. Show ranges, denominators, source/date, and what is not known.
- Design for an Indian audience where relevant: currency, units, language, geography, seasonality, and access constraints.

## Output contract

Return: executive summary; governing thought; slide-by-slide storyboard with takeaway title, evidence, visual, speaker note, and source; appendix list; likely judge objections and answers. For a PPTX, use the Anthropic PPTX skill and validate the generated file.
